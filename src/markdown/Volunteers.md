GOSIM Paris 2026 is one of the largest open-source conferences in Europe, bringing together thousands of developers, researchers, and industry leaders at Station F in Paris on May 5–6, 2026. We're looking for passionate volunteers to help make it happen.

## Why Volunteer?

- **Be part of the action** — Work alongside world-class open-source maintainers, AI researchers, and robotics engineers.
- **Expand your network** — Connect with speakers, sponsors, and attendees from across the global open-source community.
- **Gain experience** — Get hands-on event experience in one of the most exciting tech communities.
- **Free access** — Volunteers receive complimentary access to all conference sessions and activities.
- **Exclusive swag** — Receive a volunteer T-shirt and GOSIM merchandise.

## What Do Volunteers Do?

Volunteers play a key role across the event, including:

| Area | Responsibilities |
|------|-----------------|
| **Registration & Check-in** | Welcome attendees, distribute badges and materials |
| **Session Support** | Assist speakers, manage room logistics, help with A/V setup |
| **Wayfinding** | Guide attendees around Station F, answer questions |
| **Stage Coordination** | Help keep sessions on schedule across multiple tracks |
| **Community Engagement** | Support networking activities, workshops, and demos |

## Who Can Volunteer?

Anyone who is enthusiastic about open source and available in Paris on May 5–6, 2026. No prior event experience required — we provide all the training you need. Bilingual volunteers (English/French or other languages) are especially welcome.

## Time Commitment

Volunteers are expected to commit to at least one full day (May 5 or May 6). Some roles may require availability on May 4 for setup. Shift schedules will be shared in advance so you can also enjoy the conference.
