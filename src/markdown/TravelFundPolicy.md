# GOSIM Paris 2026 Travel Fund Policy

Welcome to [GOSIM Paris 2026](https://gosim.org/). Please read the Travel Fund Policy details below. If your question is not answered on this page, please contact [travel@gosim.org](mailto:travel@gosim.org) for assistance.

## Eligibility & Funding Caps

Travel funding is limited to **Confirmed Speakers** and **Spotlight Project Members** (maximum of two reimbursement quotas per Spotlight project).

To streamline processing, GOSIM provides standardized reimbursement caps based on travel distance to Paris. These caps are designed to cover standard economy-class airfare and reasonable accommodation during the event. You will only be reimbursed for actual submitted expenses up to your designated cap:

- **Tier 1 — Intercontinental Travel**: Applies to travel from outside Europe to Paris (e.g., from North America, South America, Asia, Africa, or Oceania).
- **Tier 2 — Regional Travel**: Applies to travel within Europe to Paris (e.g., from Germany, UK, Spain, Netherlands, Italy, etc.).
- Travel within Paris will not be reimbursed.

Your specific reimbursement cap will be communicated in your acceptance email.

## Reimbursement Guidelines

### General Rules

- **Event attendance is required.** Please provide proof of attendance delivering the talk (e.g., a photo) to be reimbursed.
- GOSIM reserves the right to request additional documentation to verify eligibility and expenses.
- Policy violations (submitting receipts for events not fully attended, fake receipts, or applying for overlapping event funds) will result in a permanent ban from future GOSIM travel funding.
- Book refundable travel when possible. Unused non-refundable travel will not be reimbursed if you cannot attend.

### Covered Expenses

| Category | What Is Covered | What Is Not Covered |
|----------|----------------|---------------------|
| **Airfare** | Round-trip to Paris, economy class only. Itinerary must show ticket cost and payment method. Boarding passes welcome. | Upgrades, seat selection, baggage fees, travel insurance |
| **Accommodation** | May 4–6, 2026 (event dates + 1 night prior). One room per speaker/Spotlight member. Room rate and tax only. | Meals, mini-bar, phone charges, dry cleaning, bottled water |
| **Ground Transportation** | Not covered | All local transport in Paris (taxis, rideshares, buses, trains, etc.) |

### Non-Covered Expenses

Visas · Meals · Daily commuting to Station F · Rental cars · Luxury rides · Conversion/bank fees · Any expenses incurred during personal travel days

## How to Submit Your Expenses

We do not use an external expense portal. To claim your reimbursement, please follow these steps after the event has concluded:

1. **Prepare a Summary Sheet**: Create a document listing your individual expenses and the total reimbursement amount requested. If your receipts are not in EUR, please note the conversion rate and date used.
2. **Gather Receipts**: Scan or photograph all actual receipts. They must clearly show the date, item, and payment amount. Screenshots of credit card statements are not accepted.
3. **Submit via Email**: Send your Summary Sheet and all attached receipts to [travel@gosim.org](mailto:travel@gosim.org).

**Email title format**: `GOSIM Paris 2026 Reimbursement: [Speaker/Spotlight] [Your Name] - [Total Amount in EUR] - [Departure City]`

Example: `GOSIM Paris 2026 Reimbursement: Speaker Jane Smith - €850 - Berlin`

### Important Deadlines

- Do not submit your report until the event is completely over.
- You must submit your summary and receipts **by June 20, 2026** (45 days after the event).
- Reimbursements are processed within 45 business days after your submission is received and your onsite attendance is verified.
